## Decisions waiting on a scholar (ledger status `proposed`, nothing changed)

- **mardana** (ids 24461–24475): Heading ਸਲੋਕੁ ਮਰਦਾਨਾ ੧ ॥ (Ang 553, Bihagre ki Vaar). Many commentators read these saloks as Guru Nanak Dev Ji's, addressed to Bhai Mardana (the ੧ as Mahalla 1); others attribute them to Bhai Mardana. Unchanged pending scholarly review.
- **surdas-line** (ids 53935–53935): Ang 1253: the single line ਛਾਡਿ ਮਨ ਹਰਿ ਬਿਮੁਖਨ ਕੋ ਸੰਗੁ ॥ follows Bhagat Parmanand Ji's shabad and precedes ਸਾਰੰਗ ਮਹਲਾ ੫ ਸੂਰਦਾਸ ॥; it is traditionally Bhagat Surdas Ji's one line in the Granth. No heading or signature is printed with it here. Unchanged pending scholarly review.
- **raagmala** (ids 60597–60658): Raagmala (Angs 1429-1430) carries no Mahalla; its authorship is disputed (some attribute it to the poet Alam). The current tag is inherited from Mundavani. Proposed: leave unattributed and describe the dispute. Unchanged pending scholarly review.
- **bhatt-splits** (ids 59363–59860): Per-Bhatt splits in the Swaiyye (pipeline/bhatt_attribution.json) differ from an independent edition at Angs 1394 (Kalsahar/Jalap), 1396 (Sal/Bhikha), 1401-1402 (54 lines tagged 'The Bhatts'; Gayand) and 1409 (Mathura/Haribans). Scholars count 11 or 17 Bhatts. Unchanged pending scholarly review.
- **kabir-saloks-signed-by-others** (ids 58724–58788): Inside ਸਲੋਕ ਭਗਤ ਕਬੀਰ ਜੀਉ ਕੇ, saloks 212-213 (Trilochan and Namdev in dialogue), 241 (ਕਹਿ ਨਾਮਾ) and 242 (ਸਤਿ ਭਾਖੈ ਰਵਿਦਾਸ) are signed by other Bhagats. Tagged with the collection (Bhagat Kabir Ji) as the independent edition does. Pending scholarly review.
